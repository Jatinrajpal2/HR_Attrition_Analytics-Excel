# 👥 HR Analytics Dashboard | Excel

## 📌 Introduction

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** project is developed using **Microsoft Excel** to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, job roles, job satisfaction, income, education, departments, and business travel patterns.

The dashboard provides insights into:

- Employee attrition
- Employee demographics
- Age-group analysis
- Department-wise attrition
- Education-field analysis
- Gender-wise attrition
- Business travel patterns
- Job roles
- Job satisfaction
- Average monthly income

The dashboard transforms raw HR data into interactive visualizations to support better workforce understanding and data-driven HR decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The main objective is to analyze HR data and identify:

- Employee attrition trends
- High-attrition age groups
- Department-wise attrition
- Education-field attrition patterns
- Gender-wise attrition
- Business travel and attrition patterns
- Job role-wise attrition
- Job satisfaction across job roles
- Average monthly income across job roles
- Overall employee and attrition metrics

## 🔍 Key Insights

- A total of **1,470 employees** were recorded, including **1,233 active employees** and **237 employees who left** the organization.

- Employees aged **26–35** recorded the highest attrition contribution at approximately **48.95%**, followed by the **18–25** age group at **18.57%**.

- **Research & Development** recorded the highest total attrition with **133 employees**, followed by **Sales (92)** and **Human Resources (12)**.

- Employees who **travel rarely** recorded the highest attrition with **156 employees**, followed by employees who **travel frequently (69)** and **non-travelers (12)**.

- **Male employees** accounted for approximately **63%** of total attrition, while **female employees** accounted for approximately **37%**.

- Among education fields, **Life Sciences** recorded the highest attrition with **89 employees**, followed by **Medical (63)** and **Marketing (35)**.

- The overall **attrition rate is 16.12%** based on the employee population represented in the dashboard.

- The dashboard also provides a comparison of **job roles, average job satisfaction, and average monthly income** to understand employee-level attrition patterns.
